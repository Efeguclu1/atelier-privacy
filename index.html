Privacy Policy

Atelier · Last updated: July 23, 2026

Atelier (“the App”) is a focus timer that unveils public-domain artworks as you complete sessions. This policy explains what information we handle.

Information we store

The App stores data only on your device, including:

  • Your focus preferences (default duration, haptics)
  • Which paintings you have unlocked (your collection)
  • Session completion records used to build that collection

We do not require an account and we do not operate a user database for the App.

Information we do not collect

The App does not collect personal identifiers for advertising, does not sell your data, and does not upload your collection to our servers (the current version has no backend).

Internet use

Optional links (for example, opening artwork pages at The Metropolitan Museum of Art) may leave the App and use your device browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Third-party art attribution

Artworks are provided under The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access program (and similar public-domain sources). Attribution is shown in the App. Image files are bundled or cached for offline use.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear App data by deleting the App or clearing app storage in system settings. That removes your local collection and preferences.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves (for example, if cloud sync is added later). The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Privacy Policy

Atelier · Last updated: July 25, 2026

Atelier (“the App”) is a focus timer that unveils public-domain artworks as you complete sessions. This policy explains what information we handle.

What the App stores on your device

By default everything stays on your device, including:

  • Your focus preferences (session length, daily goal, haptics)
  • Which paintings you have unlocked (your collection)
  • Session records used to build your collection, streak and practice history

You can use the entire App this way. No account is required to focus, to collect paintings, or to make a purchase.

Cloud backup (optional, off by default)

If you turn on backup in Settings, a copy of your collection, session history and preferences is stored on our behalf by Supabase, so that losing or replacing your phone does not mean losing your collection.

  • Backup begins with an anonymous account — a random identifier with no name, email or password.
  • You may optionally add an email address. We use it only to let you sign in and restore your collection on another device. We do not send marketing email.
  • Backup data is protected so that only your account can read or write it. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.

You can turn backup off at any time in Settings. To have a stored backup deleted, contact us at the address below and we will remove it.

Purchases

Optional “Patron” access is sold through the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store using the account already on your device. We never see or store your payment details.

What we do not collect

The App has no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not build an advertising profile of you. We do not collect your contacts, location or photos.

Internet use

Optional links (for example, opening artwork pages at The Metropolitan Museum of Art) leave the App and use your device browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Third-party art attribution

Artworks are provided under The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access program (and similar public-domain sources). Attribution is shown in the App. Image files are bundled with the App for offline use.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn cloud backup on or off at any time in Settings.
  • Clear all local data by deleting the App or clearing its storage in system settings.
  • Ask us to delete a stored backup by writing to the address below.

Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact
